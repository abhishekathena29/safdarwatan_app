About

"A personal story of longing, resilience, and a strong sense of duty forms the foundation of Safar Dar Watan: A Journey Home."

After relocating from the tranquil slopes of Kashmir to the vibrant metropolis of Delhi, Aania felt a profound emptiness—a separation from the customs and narratives that constituted her identity. Her sentiment was not unique; it struck a chord with others who were separated from their homeland and unable to really appreciate its wisdom and beauty.

Motivated by this common experience, Aania saw Safar Dar Watan as a means of rediscovering her roots and assisting others in rediscovering their own ties to Kashmir.

Every tale, ritual, and habit here is a step towards coming home, urging introspection, pride, and rekindling of bonds that are too important to be lost.

The platform is a rich trove of folktales that reverberate around the fire, melodies that carry the whispers of the mountains, and objects that speak of lives lived and treasured memories.
