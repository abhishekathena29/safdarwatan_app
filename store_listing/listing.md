## Full description (max 4000)
Safar Dar Watan (سفر در وطن), "A Journey Home", is a digital archive of Kashmiri heritage: the stories, customs, craft and music that make up a sense of home.

The project began with a personal story of longing. After moving from the slopes of Kashmir to Delhi, founder Aania Sayida Mir felt cut off from the customs and stories she grew up with. Many others away from the valley felt the same. Safar Dar Watan is her way of reconnecting with those roots and helping others do the same. Every tale, ritual and tradition in this archive is a step towards home.

WHAT YOU'LL FIND

• Heritage Collections: browse curated collections such as Poetry of the Valley (shayari), Traditional Cuisine (khyawun), Traditional Arts & Crafts (kārigāri), Cultural Heritage & Architecture, Oral Histories & Narratives (daastan), Music (gyavun), and Community Outreach.

• Thematic Exhibits: curated stories built from archive items, including "Naqsh… The Imprint", an exploration of Kashmir's architectural epigraphy, intricate calligraphy, stone carvings and hānkal metalwork.

• Oral Histories & Podcasts: hear young Kashmiris and community voices share their experiences of identity, environment, food, adventure and belonging.

• Documentaries & Events: read about films and gatherings such as "Hazaar Dastaan: A Thousand Stories of Srinagar's Old City", "Parihaspora: The City of Laughter" and "Gyavun: Echoes of the Valley".

• Catalogued Items: every item has its own record with its catalogue number, creator, type, duration and tags, like an index card in a real archive.

DESIGNED LIKE AN HEIRLOOM

The app is meant to feel like turning the pages of a well-loved book. It uses warm aged-paper tones, terracotta, saffron and emerald colours drawn from the Kashmiri landscape, and editorial typography.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

No account. No ads. No tracking. Safar Dar Watan does not collect any personal information.

Whether you are part of the Kashmiri diaspora, a student of South Asian culture, or simply curious, Safar Dar Watan invites you to treasure, recall and protect the memories that define home.
